Dialog Creator

Build Minecraft dialogs and get a data pack back. Poll makes a complete voting system with aligned inputs, pages, pre-filled answers and a results screen. Dialogs is the free-form editor: any type, body text and items, inputs, buttons, links between screens, pause-menu and Quick Actions hooks.
